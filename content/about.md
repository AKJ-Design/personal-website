I'm Alex. Brisbane, Australia — though regularly somewhere else in the country.

By day I lead product strategy for airport and lounge experience at a major airline. I'm not a
career software engineer, and that's the point. Everything on this site was designed, built and
shipped by me — mostly at night, mostly by seeing how far I could push an idea, then learning
enough to judge whether what came back was any good.

It started with a travel app and got out of hand. There's now a native iOS app on
TestFlight, a "brain" that holds my life's context and serves it to whatever AI tool I'm using,
and a home server in the study handling DNS, backups and monitoring for the lot. When I say I
run my life on my own APIs, that's not a slogan — my itineraries, my gym data and my server
alerts all move through software I own.

One rule keeps it honest: nothing is "done" until I can explain how it works without help. AI
wrote plenty of the code; none of it ships until I understand it. So what you'll read here is
what I actually learned — including the clunky parts, the decisions I'd take back, and the
occasional 2am rabbit hole.

The same instinct follows me away from the keyboard. I train seriously, so of course I ended up
building an automated dashboard that tracks it. More on that another time.

Here you'll find case studies and build-in-public posts, a couple a month at most, written when
there's something worth saying.

The site itself is one of the projects: Astro on Cloudflare, zero JavaScript by default, and a
design system shared with my iOS app that will carry into whatever comes next. The rejected
directions and the mocks are committed alongside the code.

If something here sparks a question, email me at hello@alexyoung.com.au or find me on
GitHub and LinkedIn. And if you're wondering whether you could build something like this
yourself… you can. It's far more approachable than you think.
