Which green carries the whole site?

Direction B gives one colour every interactive job on the site — wordmark, link underlines and marker hover, selection, card accents, and the live strip's labels. So the question isn't which swatch is nicest. It's which one survives being used that much without going loud or going grey.

What's exact and what isn't. The greens, the ground, the ink and every contrast figure are exact. The display and mono faces are stand-ins — Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono can't load here, so this uses your system faces at the intended sizes and spacing. Body copy is already the system stack, which is what Direction B specifies. Judge colour from this page, not letterforms.

Click a plate to mute the other three and pull that green up into the page furniture — the closest thing to seeing it at full strength.

What each one costs you

the honest read

#1B4332

Safest for print and Word, where rendering shifts unpredictably, and the most contrast headroom by a distance. But at this depth it reads as near-black that happens to be green — the accent thread stops announcing itself, which is most of what you're buying.

#204E39

Clears AAA both ways, so one value does links, labels, marks and filled buttons with no second "accessible variant" to maintain. Still legibly green at small mono sizes. The least interesting answer and probably the right one.

#2F5D50

Cooler and greyer — calmest next to Wayfare's warm gold if you ever show both systems together, and still AAA. But it's the least forest of the four, and drifts toward "considered neutral" rather than a key colour.

#2D6A4F

Unmistakably green, strongest thread presence at a glance. Costs you AAA (5.98:1) and leaves least margin on a projector or a cheap printer. Fine on the web; most likely to need a darker sibling for documents.
